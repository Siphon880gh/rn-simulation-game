# About this simulation

Experience the intensity of a fast-paced 12-hour shift in this nursing simulation game. Manage multiple patients in ICU and Med-Surg units, facing real workloads and emergent situations. Your goal is to complete your shift without going into overtime, testing your clinical judgment, time management, and prioritization skills in a dynamic hospital environment.

## Disclaimer

Names and scenarios are fictional. Any resemblance to actual events are coincidental.

This product is for **educational and training practice** only. It is not a substitute for supervised clinical education, facility protocols, or professional judgment at the bedside.

## Learning objectives

By the end of a shift, you should be able to:

1. **Prioritize timed work** under an accelerated military clock and limited concurrent task slots.
2. **Triage multi-patient workload** by switching clinical panels and acting on the most time-sensitive orders first.
3. **Complete clinical perform checks** (e.g. medication identity) before executing high-risk tasks.
4. **Recognize schedule risk** — overdue work, overtime pressure, and critical moments that affect patient status.
5. **Reflect on prioritization** using the end-of-shift debrief feedback.
