Why it’s harder

  • Night Med-Surg windows — meds, assessments, and slot pressure stack across the census.
  • Chaos incidents — random urgent interrupts (near-fall, desat, IV infiltrate, equipment).
  • Scripted pressure — rapid-response admit and a critical lab call land mid-shift.
